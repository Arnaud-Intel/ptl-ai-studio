# Meridian Robotics — Company Overview

Meridian Robotics designs and manufactures modular robotic arms and
autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) for small and mid-sized warehouses. The
company was founded in March 2021 in Portland, Oregon, by robotics
engineers Priya Desai and Tomas Whitfield, who previously worked together
on industrial automation systems.

## Mission

Meridian's mission is to make warehouse automation affordable for
businesses that can't justify a seven-figure automation budget. Our
flagship product line, the Atlas arm, starts at a fraction of the cost of
comparable industrial arms while matching their payload and precision
specs for light-to-medium assembly and packing tasks.

## Products

- **Atlas Arm** — a 6-axis robotic arm for pick-and-place, packing, and
  light assembly, available in three payload classes (3kg, 8kg, 16kg).
- **Wayfinder AMR** — an autonomous mobile robot that moves totes and
  pallets between warehouse zones without fixed infrastructure like
  magnetic strips or QR grids.
- **Meridian Fleet OS** — the software layer that coordinates multiple
  Atlas arms and Wayfinder robots on one warehouse floor.

## Company facts

- Headquarters: Portland, Oregon, with a growing engineering satellite
  office in Toronto
- Founded: March 2021
- Employees: 74 (as of the most recent count)
- Funding: Series B, closed January 2025, led by Cascade Ventures
- Customers: 46 warehouses across North America, primarily in apparel
  fulfillment and light electronics assembly
