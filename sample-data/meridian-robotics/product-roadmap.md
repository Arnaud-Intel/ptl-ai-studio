# Meridian Robotics — 2026 Product Roadmap

This roadmap covers planned releases for the Atlas Arm, Wayfinder AMR,
and Fleet OS product lines through the end of 2026.

## Q1 2026 (Jan–Mar)

- **Atlas Arm v4.2** — improved gripper force sensing, ships March 15,
  2026.
- **Fleet OS 3.0 beta** — opens to 5 pilot customers on February 1, 2026.

## Q2 2026 (Apr–Jun)

- **Wayfinder AMR — cold storage variant** — a version rated for
  sustained operation at -20°C, targeting a June 30, 2026 launch.
- **Fleet OS 3.0 general availability** — May 15, 2026, pending a clean
  pilot period with no major incidents.

## Q3 2026 (Jul–Sep)

- **Atlas Arm — 16kg payload class refresh** — new motor assembly,
  scheduled for September 2026.
- Begin certification testing for the European market (CE marking),
  targeting completion by the end of Q3.

## Q4 2026 (Oct–Dec)

- **Wayfinder AMR v2** — redesigned chassis and battery swap system,
  targeting a November 2026 launch ahead of the peak holiday fulfillment
  season.
- Year-end: publish the 2027 roadmap by December 15, 2026.

## Dependencies and risks

The Fleet OS 3.0 general availability date depends on the Q1 pilot
program going well — if any pilot customer reports a safety-related
incident, GA will slip to Q3 2026. The European CE certification timeline
depends on a third-party lab slot that hasn't been confirmed yet.
