# Meridian Robotics — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

This policy applies to all Meridian Robotics employees except those in
manufacturing and hardware lab roles, which require on-site presence.

## Eligibility

Employees in software, product, sales, and administrative roles may work
remotely up to 3 days per week. Full-time remote work requires manager
and HR approval and is evaluated on a role-by-role basis.

## Core hours

All employees, remote or in-office, are expected to be reachable between
10:00 AM and 3:00 PM Pacific time, to overlap with the Portland
headquarters and the Toronto engineering satellite office.

## Equipment stipend

Remote employees receive a one-time $600 home office stipend and a
company laptop. Requests for additional equipment (monitors, chairs,
etc.) go through the IT request form and are typically approved within 5
business days.

## Security requirements

Remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal tools
and must enable full-disk encryption on any device used for work,
company-owned or personal. Security training is mandatory annually, due
each employee's hire-date anniversary month.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually by HR and People Ops. The next scheduled
review is in November 2026, timed to inform any changes for the following
calendar year.
