# Q3 2026 Board Update — Meridian Robotics

Prepared by Tomas Whitfield (CEO) for the August 12, 2026 board meeting.

## Business summary

Revenue for Q2 2026 came in at $3.1M, up 18% quarter over quarter, driven
primarily by Atlas Arm sales to three new apparel-fulfillment customers.
Gross margin held steady at 46%. Customer count grew from 40 to 46
warehouses.

## Hiring

Headcount grew from 68 to 74 in Q2. Open roles remain for two hardware
engineers (Toronto office) and one enterprise sales rep (Chicago-based,
covering the Midwest). Both hardware roles are expected to close by the
end of September 2026.

## Product update

Fleet OS 3.0 is currently in pilot at two customer sites ahead of a
planned May 2026 general availability date; that date is now at risk
following a near-miss incident in July that is under review (see the
July 22 engineering meeting notes for details). The board will get a
final go/no-go recommendation on Fleet OS 3.0 GA timing by August 20,
2026.

## Risks

- The Wayfinder cold storage variant's battery performance is behind
  target; a decision on whether the June 30 launch date holds is
  expected by August 1, 2026.
- European CE certification for the Atlas Arm 16kg class depends on a
  third-party lab slot that has not yet been confirmed, putting the Q3
  certification target at risk.

## Next board meeting

The next board meeting is scheduled for November 4, 2026, where the 2027
roadmap and budget will be presented.
