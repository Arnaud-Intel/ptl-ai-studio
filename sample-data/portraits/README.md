# Portraits of nobody

Four photos for the Portrait Studio brick to restyle. **Nobody was
photographed**: FLUX.1-schnell (Apache-2.0) drew them on this project's demo
laptop on 2026-10-12, from a description of a webcam or phone picture, so
that the brick can be tried and shown without anybody's face.

| File | Asked for |
| --- | --- |
| `grey-beard-and-glasses.jpg` | a man of 45, short grey hair, round glasses, a trimmed beard, a green sweater, a bookshelf behind |
| `curls-in-the-kitchen.jpg` | a woman of 30, long curly dark hair and freckles, a yellow t-shirt, a kitchen behind, daylight |
| `blue-shirt-at-the-office.jpg` | a man of 60, bald, a short white beard, a light blue shirt, an open-plan office behind |
| `red-hoodie-on-the-sofa.jpg` | a woman of 24, a short black bob, a red hoodie, a living room behind, lamp light |

Any resemblance to a real person is the image model's chance. They are here
to be drawn over: glasses, a beard, curls, freckles, dark and light skin are
the things a restyle has to keep, and the brick's README says where it does
and where it does not.
